ROLE:

Frontend Developer and UI/UX Developer

TOOLS:

Supabase, Express.js, React, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, React-paginate, React-calendar


This project is a collaboratively developed capstone output focused on developing a Human Resource Information System for Arise Construction and Development Corporation.


This HRIS was developed in response to the locale’s reliance on manual workflows and physical records. Our team created the system to help ACDC transition from manual processes to a more efficient digital solution. The system's features are aligned with the organization’s specific needs and workflows, and it has the potential to support broader digital transformation within construction firms in the Philippines.
