PROJECT - 2024


ROLE:

Graphic Designer

TOOLS:

Canva


This project is scenario-specific; we were to design a logo and a business card based on the business's identity.


I developed this design based on a real estate theme. The logo was created from scratch, incorporating a roof shape at the top to clearly represent the industry. For the business card, I ensured consistency by using the same color palette as the logo. I also added a subtle grainy texture to symbolize real estate development, reflecting the idea that every project begins from the ground up.
